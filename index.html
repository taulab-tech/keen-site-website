SERVICE, BEFORE IT BREAKS

No spreadsheets.No guessing.No chasing operators for hours.

  1. Set the interval once.Oil and filter every 500 engine hours. Truck service every 10,000 km. Whatever your schedule is.
  2. The GPS unit counts for you.Engine hours and kilometres come off the machine. Nobody calls in a reading.
  3. Hit the interval, get a ticket.The moment a machine reaches its service interval, KeenSite opens a maintenance ticket automatically — nobody has to notice it or flag it. Upcoming turns yellow, overdue turns red, the right person is notified, and parts, suppliers and receipts stay with the ticket.
