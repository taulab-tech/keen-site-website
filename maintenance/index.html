Equipment Maintenance

Maintenance that schedules itself.

Stop chasing service dates across spreadsheets and memory. KeenSite tracks engine hours and kilometres automatically, surfaces upcoming and overdue service before it becomes a problem, and keeps every part number, supplier, receipt, and work order attached to the machine it belongs to.

The problem

Most fleets run on memory and luck.

Service history is in someone's head or a spreadsheet nobody keeps up to date. Part numbers get looked up from scratch every time. Nobody knows exactly what's due until a machine goes down — and then it's too late.

For a fleet of 15–30 machines, the weekly time spent chasing this down adds up fast. And the cost of an unplanned breakdown is almost always higher than the cost of the service that would have prevented it.

Hours lost to chasing

Checking what's due, calling suppliers, hunting down receipts. It's recurring administrative work that should be automatic.

Breakdowns that could have been prevented

When intervals are tracked by memory, things get missed. One missed oil change on a CAT 320 can mean days of downtime and a five-figure repair.

No trail when you need it

When a machine is sold, financed, or disputed, the full service record matters. Without a system, that history is gone.

How it works

Everything your fleet needs. Nothing you have to chase.

Automatic service triggers

Set intervals by engine hours or kilometres. KeenSite tracks runtime from your GPS hardware, and the instant a machine reaches its interval, a maintenance ticket opens itself — no manual check-ins, no one has to notice.

QR code issue reporting

Stick a QR code on any machine. When a crew member spots an issue, they scan it and submit a report — no app download, no account, no friction. The report lands directly on that machine's record.

Alerts before things go wrong

Upcoming service items appear in yellow when they're within range. The moment one goes overdue, it turns red and KeenSite opens a maintenance ticket automatically — the right person is notified, and it's tracked from there until it's closed out.

Every machine has its own record

One page per machine: photos, purchase date, service schedule, complete history, open issues, and attached receipts. Searchable and accessible to anyone you give access to — whether it's a mechanic, a dispatcher, or an auditor.
